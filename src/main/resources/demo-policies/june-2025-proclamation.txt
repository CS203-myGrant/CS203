Presidential Proclamation 10949
Section 2. Full Suspension of Entry.
The entry into the United States of nationals of Afghanistan, Burma, Chad, Republic of the Congo, Equatorial Guinea, Eritrea, Haiti, Iran, Libya, Somalia, Sudan, and Yemen is suspended.

Section 3. Partial Suspension of Entry.
The entry into the United States of nationals of Burundi, Cuba, Laos, Sierra Leone, Togo, Turkmenistan, and Venezuela is partially suspended.

Section 4. Scope and Implementation of Suspension.
The suspension applies to foreign nationals who are outside the United States on the effective date and do not have a valid visa on that date. The proclamation states exceptions for lawful permanent residents, dual nationals traveling on a passport issued by a non-designated country, specified visa categories, and case-by-case national-interest determinations.
