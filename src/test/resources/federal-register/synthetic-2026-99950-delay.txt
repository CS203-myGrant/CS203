Establishing a Fixed Time Period of Admission and an Extension of
Stay Procedure for Nonimmigrant Academic Students, Exchange Visitors,
and Representatives of Foreign Information Media; Delay of Effective
Date

AGENCY: U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), U.S. Department
of Homeland Security (DHS).

ACTION: Final rule; delay of effective date.

-----------------------------------------------------------------------

SUMMARY: DHS is delaying the effective date of the final rule
published in the Federal Register on July 17, 2026, which changes the
admission period for F-1 academic students and J-1 exchange visitors
from duration of status to a fixed time period, from September 15,
2026, to November 1, 2026, so that schools and sponsors can update
their systems.

DATES: The effective date of the final rule published July 17, 2026,
at 91 FR 44976, is delayed until November 1, 2026.
