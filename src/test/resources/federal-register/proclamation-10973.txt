Presidential Proclamation 10973
Restriction on Entry of Certain Nonimmigrant Workers
Section 1. Restriction on Entry.
The entry into the United States of aliens as nonimmigrants to perform services in a specialty occupation under the H-1B visa classification is restricted, except for petitions accompanied or supplemented by a $100,000 payment, subject to national-interest exceptions. The restriction applies to certain H-1B workers currently outside the United States who enter after the effective date.
